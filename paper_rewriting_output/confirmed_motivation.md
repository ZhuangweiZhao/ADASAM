# Confirmed Motivation

## Controlling Motivation

在稀缺像素标注条件下，研究者难以判断应优先投入新适配模块、数据增强还是更大模型。本文通过统一标注预算、多模型、多种子实验，区分稳定收益、架构收益和无效的稀疏化假设，并形成可复用的标签高效分割适配决策协议。

## Motivation-to-Contribution Link

This motivation makes the confirmed contribution necessary: the paper does not
present another adapter as an unquestioned improvement; it answers which sources
of low-label gains survive matched evaluation and which proposed efficiency claims
remain unsupported.

## Boundaries

The motivation is scoped to the evaluated industrial defect and remote-sensing
semantic-segmentation protocols. It does not claim universal laws, SOTA status,
real-time deployment, or cross-domain generalization without additional evidence.
